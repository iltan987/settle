Settle

Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 (automatic backup)

What Settle accesses

  • Photos and videos on your phone, with the media permissions you grant, to show them and to back them up. Location data inside photos is read so that backups are exact copies and so the app can show where a photo was taken. Backup runs automatically in the background for the folders you choose (only the camera folder unless you pick more); you can turn it off or pause it in Settings.
  • Your Google Drive, only the files Settle creates. Settle uses Google's drive.file permission. It creates a Settle folder in your Drive, uploads originals and a small amount of metadata about each item (such as when it was taken) into it, and reads back those files' size and checksums to verify the copies. It cannot see, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account's email address and Drive storage usage, read from Google Drive, to show which account is connected and how much space is left, and to resume backup once a full Drive has space again.
  • How fast your connection is. While uploading, Settle times how long it takes to open a connection to Google Drive's server, so it can slow down when your network gets busy and leave room for others. Nothing is sent in these checks and nothing about them is stored or shared.

Where your data goes

Only between your phone and your own Google Drive. Settle has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Its local index of your library stays on your phone and is excluded from Android device backups.

Settle's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Disconnect Google Drive in Settle's settings, or remove Settle's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • Uninstalling Settle deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Türkçe özet

Settle telefonundaki fotoğraf ve videolara, verdiğin izinlerle, onları göstermek ve yedeklemek için erişir. Google Drive'da yalnızca kendi oluşturduğu dosyalara erişebilir (drive.file izni); diğer dosyalarını göremez. Bağlı hesabın e-posta adresini ve Drive kullanımını yalnızca ayarlarda göstermek için okur. Veriler yalnızca telefonun ile kendi Drive'ın arasında gider: sunucu, analiz, reklam ya da takip yoktur ve hiçbir veri kimseyle paylaşılmaz. Yedekleme seçtiğin klasörler için arka planda kendiliğinden çalışır; ayarlardan kapatabilir ya da duraklatabilirsin. Yükleme sırasında ağını yormamak için Google Drive sunucusuna bağlantı kurma süresini ölçer; bu ölçümlerde veri gönderilmez ve saklanmaz. Erişimi Settle ayarlarından ya da myaccount.google.com/connections adresinden kaldırabilirsin.
