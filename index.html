Settle

A photo and video gallery for Android that backs up to your own Google Drive.

Settle shows the photos and videos on your phone in one timeline. When you ask it to, it uploads an original, byte for byte, into a Settle folder in your own Google Drive and checks that Drive's copy is identical before calling it backed up.

Settle is a personal project. It has no servers and no accounts of its own: your photos go only from your phone to your Google Drive. It asks Google only for access to the files it creates there (the drive.file permission), so it cannot see anything else in your Drive.

Read the privacy policy.
