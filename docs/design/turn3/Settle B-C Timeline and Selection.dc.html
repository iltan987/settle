B2Backup running
Behaviour. The chip shows a ring for overall progress (18 dp, pri on c3). Cells that are uploading show their own ring badge, which is the one transient status that always shows, because it is live and short. When a cell is verified, its badge fades out (200 ms). C · Selection mode
Where the actions live. The SelectionBar takes the NavBar's exact slot and height at the bottom. That puts the actions in thumb reach right after a long-press, and swapping two overlays of the same height can't move the grid. The SelectionTopBar replaces the 64 dp TopBar in place; it holds the count, Select all and overflow (Move, Add to album). Delete is the rightmost action and uses err text. Like the rest, it is undoable.
C1Long-press starts selection
close 1 selected select_all more_vert
Motion. Long-press (400 ms) gives a haptic and selects the cell, which insets 8 dp (spring 600/0.8). Every cell fades in its check ring (150 ms). Waiting and Queued badges fade in. The bars cross-fade in place (150 ms). The finger stays on the same cell because nothing moves. Day headers swap their meta for a 48 dp select-day control at the right edge, without changing height.
Strings. 1 selected / 1 seçildi · Share / Paylaş · Favourite / Favori · Back up / Yedekle · Delete / Sil. "Back up" is disabled when everything selected is already in Drive. Its long-press tooltip says: Already in Drive / Zaten Drive'da.
C2Drag to extend
close 7 selected select_all more_vert
Drag. Without lifting, dragging selects in reading order from the anchor cell to the finger. Dragging back shrinks the range. If the drag started on a selected cell, it clears instead. A 72 dp edge zone at the top and bottom auto-scrolls, faster the deeper the finger goes (shown as a faint pc wash). Each cell added gives a light tick haptic. The count updates live.
Strings. 7 selected / 7 seçildi. Spoken on lift: "7 selected".
C3Select a whole day
close 10 selected select_all more_vert
DayHeader select control. Three states: empty ring, partial (filled minus, pri) and all (filled check, pri). Tapping the control or the header text toggles: partial or empty becomes all, and all becomes none. Outside selection mode, long-pressing a header selects that day and enters selection.
Strings. Spoken: "Select Thursday, 29 October, 8 items" / "29 Ekim Perşembe'yi seç, 8 öğe" · "Yesterday, 2 of 6 selected" / "Dün, 6 öğeden 2'si seçili".
C4Mixed: some not in Drive yet
close 12 selected select_all more_vert
Mixed selection. An InfoStrip (surf, radius 16) floats above the bar whenever any selected item isn't in Drive. "Back up" gets a count badge and acts only on those 5: it skips the waiting period and starts now on Wi-Fi, even without the charger. Delete stays one tap; the snackbar then says which items weren't backed up (E5). Items in folders not chosen for backup (Not backed up) are included in Back up, after a one-time "Also back up this folder?" prompt.
