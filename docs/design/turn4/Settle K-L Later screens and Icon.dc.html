L · App icon and brand mark
Idea: settling. A dome comes to rest on two soft layers. It reads as a sun setting (backup happens at night), as a cloud resting on a phone, and as a calm layer coming to rest. It's built from one arc and two rounded bars, so it survives every mask, 24 dp and one colour.
L1Adaptive icon construction
Background layer · #1F6A64
Foreground · 108 canvas, 66 safe zone
Composed
Circle, squircle and rounded-square masks at 96, plus 48 and 24.
Geometry (108 dp canvas). Dome: an arc of radius 17 centred at (54, 49), cut flat at y = 51. Upper layer: 52×12 at (28, 54), radius 6. Lower layer: 38×10 at (35, 70), radius 5. The artwork spans x 28–80 and y 32–80, inside the 66 dp safe circle with margin, so system parallax (up to 18 dp) never clips it. The 3 dp gaps keep the shapes apart at 24 dp.
Colours. Background #1F6A64 (pri, light). Dome #F4F8F7 (bg, light). Upper layer #8ED5CC (pri, dark). Lower layer #BFEDE6 (pc, light). Ship as ic_launcher_background (solid colour) and ic_launcher_foreground (VectorDrawable), plus the monochrome layer (L2).

Themed icon. The monochrome layer is the same three shapes in a single colour. The gaps do the separating, so it reads with any tint Android applies (three wallpaper tints shown). The tile colour comes from the system.
Brand mark. The glyph plus "Settle" in Figtree 700, glyph height 1.7× the cap height, gap 0.4× the glyph. On surfaces the mark uses tonal layers. On light: dome #1F6A64 (pri), layers #5E9C95 and #9CC7C1. On dark: dome #8ED5CC (pri), layers #4FA79D and #2E6F68. Where colour is unavailable, use one colour (pri). A compact 28 dp tile version is for About (I12) and the welcome screen (A1). Minimum glyph size is 16 dp; don't recolour the layers individually.
