6 · Motion
{{ m.what }}{{ m.spec }}
Springs are given as Compose spring(stiffness, dampingRatio). Emphasized easing is cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1); standard is cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1) at 150 ms. With "Remove animations" on, every spring becomes a 100 ms fade, and the snackbar's time bar is replaced by a static "10 s" label.
7 · Iconography and status glyphs
Material Symbols Rounded, weight 400, grade 0, optical size matched to the rendered size. The filled style (FILL 1) marks active, selected and status icons; outlined is for actions. Sizes: 24 default; 20 or 18 in chips; 17 in viewer chips; 12–15 in cell badges. Every icon-only control has a content description and a long-press tooltip.
