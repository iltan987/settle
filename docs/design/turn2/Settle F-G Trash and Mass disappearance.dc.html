F5Restoring items deleted in another app
arrow_back Trash checklist
Restore from Drive. Each cell shows a 32 dp download ring. Waiting cells show an empty ring. When a file is downloaded and its checksum matches, the cell shows a check for 600 ms and then leaves the trash (collapse 250 ms). The section meta carries the total. Restores keep going if the user leaves Trash; progress shows in the Backup tab and as a silent notification.

G · Mass disappearance
Tone. Warn (amber), never error: nothing has been lost, and Settle is pausing on purpose. The copy leads with what is safe, then the likely cause, then the choices, with the safest one first and largest. The timeline notice that leads here is B9. It can't be dismissed and survives restarts until answered.
